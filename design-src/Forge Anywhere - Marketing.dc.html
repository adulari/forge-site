Practical questions

What stays free?

Everything Forge does today: CLI, TUI, daemon, local history, loopback and LAN access, direct device pairing, your own forge serve --anywhere tunnel, and workspace export/import. Anywhere only adds the managed relay, sync, and handoff.

When does the trial start?

When your first host connects — not at signup. 14 days, no card, one trial per GitHub account.

Can the service read my sessions?

No. Devices encrypt before upload and decrypt after download. The service sees routing identifiers, timestamps, sizes, object kinds, and signatures — never plaintext prompts, commands, filenames, repository names, diffs, or transcripts.

What if I lose the recovery phrase?

While you still have an authorized device, you can re-authorize others. Lose every authorized device and the phrase, and encrypted cloud content is unrecoverable — support cannot restore it. Local data is untouched.

What happens after cancellation or a failed payment?

Anywhere features stop; nothing local breaks. Local Forge, LAN access, pairing, and your own tunnels keep working.

Does Anywhere replace Git or backups?

No. Sync and handoff move Forge session state between your machines. Keep using Git and independent backups for your code.

Can my team use it?

V1 is for individual developers: one account, up to three hosts, unlimited personal devices. No seats or organizations yet.

What happens above 5 GB?

5 GB is a hard limit — new cloud sync waits until you clear space. Local Forge and local history are unaffected.

Can I keep using my own tunnel?

Yes. forge serve --anywhere stays free and works with or without a subscription.

What if the service goes offline?

Local Forge doesn't depend on the relay. Loopback, LAN, direct pairing, and self-managed tunnels keep working while it's down.
