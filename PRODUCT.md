# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Individual developers who use Forge locally and want to understand its model-agnostic workflow or securely continue a live Forge session away from their primary machine.

## Product Purpose

Present Forge as a free, open-source coding agent and Forge Anywhere as its optional paid remote companion. Help visitors evaluate the product, verify the free/paid boundary, install Forge, or start a no-card Anywhere trial with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Mechanical, candid, and energetic. The site should feel like a well-used workshop instrument: technically precise, visually warm, and direct about tradeoffs.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic blue SaaS layouts, soft gradient clouds, decorative glass cards, stock-photo developer scenes, vague security claims, and pricing that obscures what remains free. Preserve the production Forge orange, cream, near-black, Bricolage Grotesque, Geist, and terminal/workshop motifs.

## Design Principles

1. Show the product working through terminal and device surfaces instead of abstract claims.
2. State the commercial boundary plainly: Forge remains free; Anywhere is optional.
3. Let Forge orange carry the identity, with dark metal and cream work surfaces for contrast.
4. Make trust inspectable through specific encryption, recovery, retention, and limit details.
5. Keep every conversion path legible, reversible, and free of behavioral tracking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, sufficient contrast, 200% zoom, responsive layouts, reduced motion, and color-independent status cues. Avoid requiring animation, hover, or fine pointer control to understand or use the site.
