Questions before you connect a host.

Does Forge become paid?

No. Forge itself, local and LAN access, direct pairing, and user-managed tunnels remain free and unchanged. Forge Anywhere pays for the managed relay, encrypted cloud storage, sync, fleet, jobs, handoff, and replay links.

When does the trial start?

The 14-day no-card trial begins when your first host connects, not when you sign in. One trial is available per GitHub account.

Can the service read my prompts or code?

Encrypted payload contents are readable only by authorized devices. The service processes routing identifiers, timestamps, sizes, envelope kinds, sequences, and signatures. It does not receive the local Forge daemon token.

What happens if I lose my recovery words?

An already-authorized device can enroll another device. If every authorized device and the 24 recovery words are lost, Forge cannot decrypt or recover the cloud history.

What happens after cancellation or expiry?

Paid access continues through the paid-through date. Read-only access allows download, restore, delete, export, and billing changes. Encrypted cloud data is retained for up to 90 days after suspension, with warnings before deletion.

Are teams supported?

Not in version one. Forge Anywhere is for individual developers and personal devices. Team administration and commercial single-tenant hosting are future products.

How large can a workspace handoff be?

A compressed capsule can be up to 100 MB, with individual files up to 25 MB. Unsafe paths, links, devices, secrets, ignored caches, and build output are rejected with a visible explanation.
