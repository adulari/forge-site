Try it before paying

Forge Anywhere includes a 14-day trial without a card. The trial begins only when your first host connects, so you can evaluate the relay, sync, mobile access, and handoff before subscribing. One trial is available per GitHub account.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time through the billing portal. Cancellation stops the next renewal and does not remove access for the period already paid. You keep full service through the paid-through date.

After paid access ends, read-only access remains available for download, restore, deletion, export, and billing changes before the published retention deadline.

Consumer withdrawal rights

If consumer law gives you a cooling-off or withdrawal right, that right applies in addition to this policy. Starting Forge Anywhere during a statutory withdrawal period may require your express request for immediate digital service. Any lawful deduction for service already supplied will be explained at checkout.

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory rights in your country.

When we issue refunds

We issue refunds when required by law and for duplicate or incorrect charges. We will also review requests made within 14 days of an initial purchase or annual renewal, particularly when the account has little or no paid-period use.

For prolonged service failures caused by Forge Anywhere, we may provide a proportional refund or service credit. We normally do not refund elapsed subscription time after substantial use, failures of local Forge or third-party providers, loss of recovery words, or account suspension for a material terms violation.

How to request a refund

Use Paddle's receipt or buyer support link, or email billing@adulari.dev from the address associated with your GitHub account. Include the Paddle transaction number and the reason for the request. Do not send card details, recovery words, tokens, prompts, code, or repository content.

Paddle is the merchant of record and returns approved funds to the original payment method.

Review and payment timing

We aim to review complete requests within five business days. Once approved and sent by Paddle, a bank or payment provider may take additional business days to show the refund. Taxes are refunded as required by the original transaction and applicable law.

A refund may end the associated paid entitlement. Export anything you need before requesting account deletion.

Forge stays open source and free. Anywhere is optional.
